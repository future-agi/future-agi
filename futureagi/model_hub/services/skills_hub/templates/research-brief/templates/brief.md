# Decision brief

## Decision and scope

State the question, intended reader, decision date, and constraints. Explain what
is deliberately outside scope and how success will be measured.

## Recommendation

Give the recommended option in one sentence, then explain the key trade-off.
State confidence and the condition that would change the recommendation.

## Options

Compare the current approach and realistic alternatives against the same criteria.
Separate unknown values from measured zeroes. Include cost, reversibility, and
failure behavior only where evidence supports them.

## Strongest objection

Present the best case against the recommendation, not a weak alternative chosen
to make it look good. Explain why the recommendation still holds or revise it.

## Open questions and next check

For each material unknown, state the question, how its answer could change the
choice, and the smallest verification step. Do not invent owners or deadlines.

## Sources

List the primary sources actually consulted, with locators and access dates.
Identify unavailable sources and secondary summaries explicitly.
