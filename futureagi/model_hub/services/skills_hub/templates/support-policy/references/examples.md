# Edge cases

- Delivered 30 days ago, unused, no box: within the standard window. Confirm the
  order reference; missing packaging alone does not disqualify the return.
- Purchased 40 days ago, delivered 12 days ago: use the delivery date. Do not reject
  based on purchase date.
- Delivered 31 days ago, wrong item: route through damaged or incorrect goods
  review rather than applying the standard-window rejection.
- Customer already disputed the payment: escalate before any refund action to
  avoid duplicate reimbursement.
- A download was purchased but access is unknown: ask whether it was accessed;
  do not infer access from purchase alone.
- The customer quotes a different public policy: preserve the source and date,
  flag the conflict, and request human review rather than silently picking one.
