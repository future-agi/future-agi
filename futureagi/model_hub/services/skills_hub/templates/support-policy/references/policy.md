# Sample return policy

Replace this sample with the team's approved policy before customer use.

## Standard return

Unused physical goods may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery,
including day 30. The customer supplies a receipt or a verifiable order reference.
Original packaging is preferred but is not a condition of eligibility. Refunds
follow inspection and go to the original payment method; do not promise a posting
date without confirmation from the payment provider.

## Damaged or incorrect goods

Collect a description of the issue and, if the customer is comfortable sharing
one, a photo with personal details removed. Escalate for replacement or refund
review even if the standard return window has passed. Do not treat this clause
as automatic approval or require the customer to ship an unsafe item.

## Exclusions and conflicts

Digital downloads already accessed, personalized goods, and final-sale goods
require human review. A chargeback, duplicate refund, missing delivery record,
or a conflicting local consumer right also requires human review. Mandatory
consumer rights take precedence over this sample; a qualified person decides
how they apply.

## Shipping and timing

Do not invent return shipping charges, warehouse addresses, inspection dates, or
processing deadlines. Obtain them from the approved order workflow. Until a
refund transaction is confirmed, say 'eligible for review', not 'refunded'.
