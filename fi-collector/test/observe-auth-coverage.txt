Spec: e2e/flows/observe/collector-personal-key-lifecycle.spec.ts
Goal: a session-created personal key accepts OTLP and its trace appears in Observe;
retained synthetic system and retired credentials are refused; a deleted personal
key is refused within 30 seconds measured from BEFORE the deleting request.
The earlier monotonic boundary conservatively bounds latency from the later DB
commit. Primary Postgres confirms deletion before export attempts. A still-eligible
control proves collector liveness. Exact identities are compared in Observe,
the real span-list API and spans/traces FINAL, with a full 15-second exclusion
window after a positive storage barrier. Attachments contain IDs/status/timing only.

The split harness extension provides an E2E-image-only retained-state fixture,
valid synthetic hashes and independent primary readback, plus bounded OTLP HTTP
results without response-body/header logging. It is not a production issuer.
Actor provisioning is the session-personal dependency from L1; the lane does not
fall back to GET /accounts/keys/ or an automatically created credential.

Owed on the composed personal-provisioning head: attach iteration, built-image
managed run, one-sided fallibility for all four backend checks, restored managed
green, and the final catalog/typecheck. The owned runner and exact plan are recorded
in the lane result; static success does not constitute browser coverage acceptance.

Independent remaining obligations: sandbox/platform issuance -> collector ->
correctly scoped storage/Observe; publication-loss, replica-lag and reconnect are
backend controls, not prerequisites for the reachable browser journey.
